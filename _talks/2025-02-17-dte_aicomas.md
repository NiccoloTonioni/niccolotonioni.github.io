In this study we explore neural compression techniques, including autoencoders (AEs) and variational autoencoders (VAEs), for reduced-order modeling of experimental datasets. By comparing these methods with traditional approaches like Proper Orthogonal Decomposition (POD), we demonstrate that convolutional neural networks can effectively reconstruct previously unseen flow regimes while compressing data into just three variables, enabling interpretable and efficient modeling.
